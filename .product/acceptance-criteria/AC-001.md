# AC-001: Turnip slot shows Turnip crop info

- **Status:** Deprecated
- **User Story:** [US-001](../user-stories/US-001.md)

## Criterion

On the recipes page, hovering the "Turnip" ingredient slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card shows exactly "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". The store names come from the Turnip "Seed Locations" on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html, reduced to store names only.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, find the Turnip Salad card, and hover the pointer over its "Turnip" slot. Expected: the info text "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" is visible.

## Evidence

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app`. The Cursor browser tool was unavailable (every call returned "Server not found"), so I drove a real headless Google Chrome through the DevTools protocol with a throwaway script (`tmp/hover_check.py`, git-ignored). It opened `http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false` (what the form submits with "Owned only" unchecked), scrolled the Turnip Salad card's "1× Turnip" slot into view, moved the real mouse pointer onto its center (`Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseMoved), listed every info text that was rendered visible, and took a screenshot (`tmp/ac001_turnip_hover.png`).
- **Result:** The element under the pointer was the "1× Turnip" slot button. Exactly one info text was visible: "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". The screenshot shows it in a box just below the Turnip slot on the Turnip Salad card.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** subagent (US-001)
