# AC-003: Turnip item in the slot popup shows Turnip crop info

- **Status:** Deprecated
- **User Story:** [US-001](../user-stories/US-001.md)

## Criterion

After clicking the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card to open its popup list, hovering the "Turnip" item in that list shows exactly "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" (source: Turnip "Seed Locations" on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html).

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, then hover the pointer over the "Turnip" entry in the popup list. Expected: the info text "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" is visible.

## Evidence

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app`. The Cursor browser tool was unavailable ("Server not found"), so I drove a real headless Google Chrome through the DevTools protocol (`tmp/hover_check.py`, git-ignored). It opened `http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false`, clicked the Turnip Salad card's "1× Turnip" slot with real mouse press and release events, then moved the real mouse pointer onto the "Turnip" entry in the popup list (`Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseMoved), listed every visible info text, and took a screenshot (`tmp/ac003_popup_turnip_hover.png`).
- **Result:** Exactly one info text was visible: "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". The screenshot shows it directly under the "Turnip ×7" row in the popup titled "Turnip".
- **Outcome:** Pass
