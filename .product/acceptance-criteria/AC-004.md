# AC-004: Rare variety in the slot popup shows its base crop

- **Status:** Deprecated
- **User Story:** [US-001](../user-stories/US-001.md)

## Criterion

In the popup list opened from the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card, hovering the "Giant Turnip" item shows exactly "Season: Spring; Rare variety of Turnip", with no store names (source: Turnip "Rare Variety" on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html).

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, then hover the pointer over the "Giant Turnip" entry in the popup list. Expected: the info text "Season: Spring; Rare variety of Turnip" is visible and contains no store name.

## Evidence

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app`. The Cursor browser tool was unavailable ("Server not found"), so I drove a real headless Google Chrome through the DevTools protocol (`tmp/hover_check.py`, git-ignored). It opened `http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false`, clicked the Turnip Salad card's "1× Turnip" slot with real mouse press and release events, then moved the real mouse pointer onto the "Giant Turnip" entry in the popup list (`Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseMoved), listed every visible info text, and took a screenshot (`tmp/ac004_popup_giant_turnip_hover.png`).
- **Result:** Exactly one info text was visible: "Season: Spring; Rare variety of Turnip". It contains no store name. The screenshot shows it directly under the "Giant Turnip" row in the popup.
- **Outcome:** Pass
