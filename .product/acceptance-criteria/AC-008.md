# AC-008: Clicking a slot still opens its popup list

- **Status:** Deprecated
- **User Story:** [US-001](../user-stories/US-001.md)

## Criterion

Clicking the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card still opens the slot popup list, titled "Turnip", listing the items that can fill it (Turnip and Giant Turnip).

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad. Expected: a popup titled "Turnip" opens and lists "Turnip" and "Giant Turnip".

## Evidence

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app`. The Cursor browser tool was unavailable ("Server not found"), so I drove a real headless Google Chrome through the DevTools protocol (`tmp/hover_check.py`, git-ignored). It opened `http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false`, moved the pointer onto the Turnip Salad card's "1× Turnip" slot, and clicked it with real mouse press and release events (`Input.dispatchMouseEvent`). It then read whether the slot popup was open, its title, and its list entries, and took a screenshot (`tmp/ac008_popup.png`).
- **Result:** The popup was open, titled "Turnip", with "Need 1 · 2 items can fill this slot", and listed "Turnip" and "Giant Turnip". The screenshot shows the open popup over the page.
- **Outcome:** Pass
