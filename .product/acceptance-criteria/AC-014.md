# AC-014: Turnip slot hover shows Turnip crop info

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-004](../user-stories/US-004.md)

## Criterion

On the recipes page, hovering the "Turnip" ingredient slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card shows exactly "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" (source: Turnip "Seed Locations" on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html, reduced to store names).

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and hover the pointer over the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad card. Expected: the info text "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" is visible.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and drove headless Google Chrome 154 through its DevTools protocol (throwaway script `tmp/us004_verify.py`). Opened http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false and confirmed the "Owned only" checkbox was unchecked. Scrolled the Turnip Salad card into view and sent a real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseMoved to the center of its "1× Turnip" slot. Confirmed with `elementFromPoint` that the pointer was over that slot, then listed every crop info text on the page that was rendered visible. Screenshot: `tmp/us004_ac014.png`.
- **Result:** Pointer over the Turnip slot. Exactly one visible info text on the page, on the Turnip Salad card: "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". Same result on a second run of the script.
