# AC-016: Group slot hover shows no info

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-004](../user-stories/US-004.md)

## Criterion

On the recipes page, hovering the "Vegetable A" ingredient slot on the Mixed Salad recipe card shows exactly "No info available".

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and hover the pointer over the "Vegetable A" slot on the Mixed Salad card. Expected: the info text "No info available" is visible.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and drove headless Google Chrome 154 through its DevTools protocol (throwaway script `tmp/us004_verify.py`). Opened http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false and confirmed the "Owned only" checkbox was unchecked. Scrolled the Mixed Salad card into view and sent a real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseMoved to the center of its "1× Vegetable A" slot. Confirmed with `elementFromPoint` that the pointer was over that slot, then listed every crop info text on the page that was rendered visible. Screenshot: `tmp/us004_ac016.png`.
- **Result:** Pointer over the Vegetable A slot. Exactly one visible info text on the page, on the Mixed Salad card: "No info available". Same result on a second run of the script.
