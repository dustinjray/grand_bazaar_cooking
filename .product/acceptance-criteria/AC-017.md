# AC-017: Non-spring slot hover shows no info

- **Status:** Deprecated
- **User Story:** [US-004](../user-stories/US-004.md)

## Criterion

On the recipes page, hovering the "Tomato" ingredient slot on the Mixed Salad recipe card shows exactly "No info available". Tomato is not listed on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html.

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and hover the pointer over the "Tomato" slot on the Mixed Salad card. Expected: the info text "No info available" is visible.
