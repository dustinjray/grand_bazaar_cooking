# AC-018: Card info hides when the pointer moves away

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-004](../user-stories/US-004.md)

## Criterion

No info text is visible on the Turnip Salad card before its "Turnip" slot is hovered. After hovering the slot and then moving the pointer away from it, the info text is no longer visible.

## Verification Method

Start the app and open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked. With the pointer away from all slots, confirm no info text is visible on the Turnip Salad card. Hover its "Turnip" slot and confirm the info text appears, then move the pointer to an empty area of the page. Expected: no info text before hovering, the text while hovering, and no info text after the pointer moves away.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and drove headless Google Chrome 154 through its DevTools protocol (throwaway script `tmp/us004_verify.py`). Opened http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false and confirmed the "Owned only" checkbox was unchecked. Scrolled the Turnip Salad card into view. Sent real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseMoved events: first to an empty area (point (1329, 5) on the blank part of the site header, where `elementFromPoint` returned the header itself and no control or card), then to the center of the card's "1× Turnip" slot, then back to the same empty area. After each move, listed every crop info text on the page that was rendered visible. Screenshots: `tmp/us004_ac018_before.png`, `tmp/us004_ac018_during.png`, `tmp/us004_ac018_after.png`.
- **Result:** Before hovering: no visible info text anywhere on the page. While hovering the Turnip slot: one visible text on Turnip Salad, "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". After moving away: no visible info text anywhere on the page. Same result on a second run of the script.
- **Outcome:** Pass
