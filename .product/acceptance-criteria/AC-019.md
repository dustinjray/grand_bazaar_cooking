# AC-019: Clicking a slot still opens its popup list

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-004](../user-stories/US-004.md)

## Criterion

Clicking the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card opens the slot popup titled "Turnip", listing "Turnip" and "Giant Turnip".

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad. Expected: a popup titled "Turnip" opens and lists "Turnip" and "Giant Turnip".

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and drove headless Google Chrome 154 through its DevTools protocol (throwaway script `tmp/us004_verify.py`). Opened http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false and confirmed the "Owned only" checkbox was unchecked. Scrolled the Turnip Salad card into view and sent a real left click (`Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseMoved, mousePressed, mouseReleased) at the center of its "1× Turnip" slot. Logged page mouse events, then read the slot popup's open state, title, and item rows. Screenshot: `tmp/us004_ac019.png`.
- **Result:** Page received mousedown, mouseup, and click on the slot button. Popup open: true. Title: "Turnip". Subtitle: "Need 1 · 2 items can fill this slot". Rows: "Turnip" (×7), "Giant Turnip". No rows expanded. Same result on a second run of the script. An earlier, first run of the script reported the popup as not open (title still "Ingredient", no rows). That run had no event logging, so the cause was not determined. It may be the script's page-load race, which was fixed afterwards (it checked readiness before navigation had started). The two later runs, with the fix and event logging, both passed.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** subagent (US-004)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

### Entry 2

- **Checked:** The orchestrator's own script, `tmp/orch_verify2.py`, run three times: twice after US-005 and once after US-004's subagent finished. Each time it clicked the Turnip Salad "Turnip" slot with a real mouse click, then read the popup's open state, title, and rows.
- **Result:** On all three runs the popup was open, titled "Turnip", and listed the rows "Turnip" and "Giant Turnip".
