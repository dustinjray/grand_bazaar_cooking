# AC-022: Clicking a popup row expands its indented info

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card, clicking the "Turnip" row shows exactly "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" beneath the "Turnip" name, and the text starts further to the right than the name (indented). Source: Turnip "Seed Locations" on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, then click the "Turnip" row in the popup. Compare the positions of the row's name and its info text. Expected: the info text "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" is visible, sits below the "Turnip" name, and its left edge is to the right of the name's left edge.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us005_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` moves and clicks). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, then clicked the center of the "Turnip" row in the popup. It then read the bounding box of the row's name, and of the info text (a DOM Range over the text, so padding does not count). Screenshot: `tmp/us005_ac022.png`, checked by eye.
- **Result:** The Turnip row was `aria-expanded=true`, and its visible info text was exactly "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". The name box was left=446.08, top=420, bottom=441. The info text box was left=462.08, top=445: below the name (445 > 441) and 16px to the right of the name's left edge. The Giant Turnip row stayed collapsed and the popup stayed open. The screenshot shows the text on the line under "Turnip", indented. No JS exceptions.
- **Outcome:** Pass
