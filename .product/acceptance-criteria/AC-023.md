# AC-023: Clicking an expanded popup row collapses it

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card, after the "Turnip" row has been expanded by clicking it, clicking it again hides its info text.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, click the "Turnip" row in the popup and confirm its info text is visible, then click the "Turnip" row again. Expected: after the second click, no info text is visible for the "Turnip" row.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us005_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, clicked the "Turnip" row, read the popup, clicked the "Turnip" row again, and read the popup again. Screenshot after the second click: `tmp/us005_ac023.png`.
- **Result:** After the first click, the Turnip row was `aria-expanded=true` and showed "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". After the second click, the Turnip row was `aria-expanded=false` with no visible info text, and the dialog `innerText` contained no "Season". The popup stayed open throughout. No JS exceptions.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** subagent (US-005)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
