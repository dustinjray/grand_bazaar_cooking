# AC-024: Rare variety row expands to its base crop

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card, clicking the "Giant Turnip" row shows exactly "Season: Spring; Rare variety of Turnip", with no store names (source: Turnip "Rare Variety" on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html).

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, then click the "Giant Turnip" row in the popup. Expected: the info text "Season: Spring; Rare variety of Turnip" is visible for that row and contains no store name.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us005_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, then clicked the "Giant Turnip" row in the popup and read the visible info text. Screenshot: `tmp/us005_ac024.png`.
- **Result:** The Giant Turnip row was `aria-expanded=true`, and its only visible info text was exactly "Season: Spring; Rare variety of Turnip". That text contains no store name: neither "Miguel's Mercantile" nor "Happy Shoppe", nor "Purchase at". The Turnip row stayed collapsed. No JS exceptions.
