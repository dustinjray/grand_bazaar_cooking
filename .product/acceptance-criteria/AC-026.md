# AC-026: Popup rows expand independently

- **Status:** Deprecated
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Vegetable A" slot on the Mixed Salad recipe card, after clicking the "Turnip" row and then the "Onion" row, both rows' info texts are visible ("Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" and "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile"). Clicking the "Turnip" row again hides the Turnip text while the Onion text stays visible. Source: https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and click the "Vegetable A" slot on Mixed Salad. Click the "Turnip" row, then the "Onion" row, and note the visible info texts. Then click the "Turnip" row again and note them again. Expected: after the first two clicks, both texts are visible. After the third click, only "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" (Onion) is visible.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us005_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Vegetable A" slot on Mixed Salad, clicked the "Turnip" row, then the "Onion" row, and read every row's rendered info text. It then clicked the "Turnip" row again and read them again. Each row was scrolled into view in the list before it was clicked. Screenshots: `tmp/us005_ac026_two.png` and `tmp/us005_ac026_three.png`.
- **Result:** After the first two clicks, exactly two rows had rendered info text: Onion showed "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile", and Turnip showed "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". Both rows were `aria-expanded=true`. In the screenshot, the Onion text is in view, and the Turnip row sits lower in the list's scroll area, so it is rendered and not hidden. After the third click, only Onion's "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" was rendered; Turnip was `aria-expanded=false` with no info. The popup stayed open throughout. No JS exceptions.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** subagent (US-005)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

- **Result:** After the first two clicks, both "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" and "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" were visible. After collapsing Turnip, only "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" (Onion) was visible.
