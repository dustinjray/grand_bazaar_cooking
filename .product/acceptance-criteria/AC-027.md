# AC-027: Popup rows are collapsed again after reopening

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

After the "Turnip" row is expanded in the popup for the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card, closing the popup and opening it again from the same slot shows no expanded rows (no info text visible).

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, click the "Turnip" row and confirm its info text is visible, close the popup with its Close button, then click the "Turnip" slot again. Expected: the reopened popup lists "Turnip" and "Giant Turnip" with no info text visible.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us005_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad, clicked the "Turnip" row, and read the popup. It then clicked the popup's Close button, read `#slot-dialog.open`, clicked the same "Turnip" slot again, and read the reopened popup. Screenshot of the reopened popup: `tmp/us005_ac027.png`.
- **Result:** After the row click, Turnip was expanded and showed "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". After the Close click, the dialog was closed (`open=false`). The reopened popup was titled "Turnip" and listed "Turnip ×7" and "Giant Turnip", both `aria-expanded=false` with no visible info text. The dialog `innerText` contained neither "Season" nor "No info". No JS exceptions.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** subagent (US-005)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

### Entry 2

- **Checked:** The orchestrator's own script, `tmp/orch_verify2.py`. In the Turnip Salad "Turnip" popup, it clicked the "Turnip" row and confirmed the Turnip text was visible. It then clicked the popup's Close button with a real mouse click, confirmed the popup had closed, and clicked the Turnip Salad "Turnip" slot again. Finally it listed the rows and the visible info texts.
- **Result:** Before closing, the Turnip text was visible, and the popup did close. After reopening, the rows were "Turnip" and "Giant Turnip" and no info text was visible.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** orchestrator
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
