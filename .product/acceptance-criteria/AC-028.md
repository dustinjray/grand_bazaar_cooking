- **Status:** Verified

## Criterion

Every crop on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html that is an app ingredient shows the expected info text on the recipes page. The text appears either by hovering a recipe-card slot with that exact name, or by clicking a slot popup row with that exact name to expand it. ## Verification Method

Start the app and open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked. For each ingredient in the table, either hover a recipe-card slot with that exact name, or open a slot popup that lists it and click its row to expand it. Expected: the visible info text matches the table exactly for all 22 ingredients.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** The orchestrator's own script, `tmp/orch_verify2.py`, run after US-005's popup change. It started the app and headless Google Chrome and opened `http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false`. For each of the 22 ingredients it hovered, with the real pointer, a recipe-card slot with that exact name. If no such slot exists, it clicked a slot whose popup lists the ingredient, then clicked that row to expand it. It then compared the visible info text with the table.
- **Result:** 22 of 22 matched exactly.
  - By card hover (13): the 12 base crops and Star Potato.
  - By popup expand (9): Giant Turnip (Vegetable A popup), Twisted Asparagus, Giant Cabbage, Brawny Onion, Calabash Radish, Fork-Root Daikon, Golden Garlic and Pom-pom Broccoli (each in its base crop's popup), and Golden Strawberry (Fruit B popup).
