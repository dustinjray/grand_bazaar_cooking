# AC-031: Multi-season popup row shows every season

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Vegetable A" slot on the Mixed Salad recipe card, clicking the "Tomato" row shows exactly "Season: Summer, Autumn; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile". Source: Tomato on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_summer.html and https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_autumn.html.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Vegetable A" slot on Mixed Salad, then click the "Tomato" row in the popup. Expected: the info text "Season: Summer, Autumn; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" is visible for that row.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Ran `python tmp/us005b_verify.py`. It starts the app in-process (`app.main:app` under uvicorn on 127.0.0.1:8015) and headless Google Chrome (DevTools port 9415, temp profile), and drives Chrome with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` moves and clicks on http://127.0.0.1:8015/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), starting each check from a fresh page load. "Visible info text" means a `.filler-info` element in a popup row that passes `checkVisibility()`, has nonzero height, and has text. Output is in `tmp/us005b_results.json`. It clicked the "Vegetable A" slot on Mixed Salad, then clicked the "Tomato" row and listed the visible info texts. Screenshot: `tmp/us005b_ac031.png`.
- **Result:** The popup was titled "Vegetable A" with 20 rows, including "Tomato". The Tomato row was `aria-expanded=true`, and the only visible info text was exactly "Season: Summer, Autumn; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile", on the Tomato row. No JS exceptions.
