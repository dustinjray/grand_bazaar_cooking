# AC-032: Multi-season rare variety row shows its base crop's seasons

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Vegetable A" slot on the Mixed Salad recipe card, clicking the "Giant Tomato" row shows exactly "Season: Summer, Autumn; Rare variety of Tomato", with no store names. Source: Tomato's "Rare Variety: Giant Tomato" on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_summer.html and https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_autumn.html.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Vegetable A" slot on Mixed Salad, then click the "Giant Tomato" row in the popup. Expected: the info text "Season: Summer, Autumn; Rare variety of Tomato" is visible for that row and contains no store name.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Ran `python tmp/us005b_verify.py`. It starts the app in-process (`app.main:app` under uvicorn on 127.0.0.1:8015) and headless Google Chrome (DevTools port 9415, temp profile), and drives Chrome with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` moves and clicks on http://127.0.0.1:8015/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), starting each check from a fresh page load. "Visible info text" means a `.filler-info` element in a popup row that passes `checkVisibility()`, has nonzero height, and has text. Output is in `tmp/us005b_results.json`. It clicked the "Vegetable A" slot on Mixed Salad, then clicked the "Giant Tomato" row, listed the visible info texts, and searched them for every store name in `data/crops.json` (Happy Shoppe, Miguel's Mercantile, Paradise Sundries).
