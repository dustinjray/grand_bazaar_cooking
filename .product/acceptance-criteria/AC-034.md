# AC-034: Popup rows from different seasons expand independently

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-005](../user-stories/US-005.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Vegetable A" slot on the Mixed Salad recipe card, after clicking the "Turnip" row and then the "Pumpkin" row, both rows' info texts are visible ("Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" and "Season: Summer; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile"). Clicking the "Turnip" row again hides the Turnip text while the Pumpkin text stays visible. Sources: Turnip on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_spring.html; Pumpkin on https://fogu.com/sos6/farming/crops_summer.html only.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and click the "Vegetable A" slot on Mixed Salad. Click the "Turnip" row, then the "Pumpkin" row, and note the visible info texts. Then click the "Turnip" row again and note them again. Expected: after the first two clicks, both texts are visible. After the third click, only "Season: Summer; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" (Pumpkin) is visible.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Ran `python tmp/us005b_verify.py`. It starts the app in-process (`app.main:app` under uvicorn on 127.0.0.1:8015) and headless Google Chrome (DevTools port 9415, temp profile), and drives Chrome with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` moves and clicks on http://127.0.0.1:8015/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), starting each check from a fresh page load. After the third click, only Pumpkin "Season: Summer; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" was visible. The popup stayed open. No JS exceptions.
