## Verification Method

Start the app and open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked. For each ingredient in the table, either hover a recipe-card slot with that exact name, or open a slot popup that lists it and click its row to expand it. Expected: the visible info text matches the table exactly for all 61 ingredients.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Script `tmp/us006_verify.py monitor`, run after `data/crops.json` was regenerated with `python scrape_crops.py` from all four season pages. It started the real app with `python -m app`, launched headless Google Chrome, and opened `http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false`. The "Owned only" checkbox was read back as unchecked, and 266 recipe cards were listed. The expected texts were read directly from this criterion's table (61 rows). For each ingredient, the script hovered a recipe-card slot with that exact name using the real pointer. If no such slot exists, it clicked a slot whose popup lists the ingredient, checked that no row was expanded, then clicked that row to expand it. It then compared the visible info text with the table. Full output: `tmp/us006_monitor.json`.
- **Result:** 61 of 61 matched exactly, each showing exactly one visible text.
  - By card hover (38): the 32 base crops, plus Star Potato, Heart Watermelon, Jewel Pineapple, Giant Tomato, Curled Chili Pepper, and Three-Forked Carrot.
  - By popup expand (23): Giant Turnip and Wavy Cucumber (Vegetable A popup), Golden Strawberry and Jewel Melon (Fruit B popup), Giant Pumpkin and Dancing Red Bell Pepper (Vegetable C popup), and the other 17 in their base crop's popup. Every popup opened with all rows collapsed.
  - Examples observed: Potato "Season: Spring, Summer, Winter; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile", Giant Tomato "Season: Summer, Autumn; Rare variety of Tomato", Sweet Potato "Season: Autumn; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" (fogu spells it "Miguel's Mercantie" on that row), and Napa Cabbage "Season: Winter; Purchase at Paradise Sundries, Happy Shoppe".
- **Outcome:** Pass

- **Date:** 2026-09-27
