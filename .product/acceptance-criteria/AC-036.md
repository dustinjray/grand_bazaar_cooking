# AC-036: Hovering a popup fish row shows no info

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-007](../user-stories/US-007.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Fish A" slot on the Fish & Chips recipe card, hovering the "Icefish" row shows no info text.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, and hover the pointer over the "Icefish" row without clicking it. Expected: no info text is visible in the popup.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us007_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome 154.0.8037.57 via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` input; this criterion used its own fresh page load). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, then sent a mouse move (no click) to the center of the "Icefish" row in the popup, at (408.45, 371.61). It used `elementFromPoint` to confirm the pointer was over the Icefish row, then read all rendered info text in the popup. Screenshot: `tmp/us007_ac036.png`, checked by eye.
- **Result:** The element under the pointer belonged to the row named "Icefish", and that row matched `:hover`. All rows stayed `aria-expanded=false` with no visible info text. The dialog `innerText` contained neither "Season" nor "No info".
- **Outcome:** Pass
