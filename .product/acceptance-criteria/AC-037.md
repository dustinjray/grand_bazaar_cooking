# AC-037: Clicking a popup fish row expands its indented info

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-007](../user-stories/US-007.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Fish A" slot on the Fish & Chips recipe card, clicking the "Icefish" row shows exactly "Seasons: Spring, Winter; Weather: All weather; Locations: Bazaar, Gabriel's House; Rod: Gold" beneath the "Icefish" name, and the text starts further to the right than the name (indented). Source: Icefish row on https://fogu.com/sos6/activities/fishing.html.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, then click the "Icefish" row in the popup. Compare the positions of the row's name and its info text. Expected: the info text "Seasons: Spring, Winter; Weather: All weather; Locations: Bazaar, Gabriel's House; Rod: Gold" is visible, sits below the "Icefish" name, and its left edge is to the right of the name's left edge.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us007_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome 154.0.8037.57 via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks; this criterion used its own fresh page load). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, then clicked the "Icefish" row in the popup. It compared the bounding box of the row's name with a DOM Range over the info text (so padding does not count). Screenshot: `tmp/us007_ac037.png`, checked by eye.
- **Result:** The Icefish row was `aria-expanded=true`, and its visible info text was exactly "Seasons: Spring, Winter; Weather: All weather; Locations: Bazaar, Gabriel's House; Rod: Gold". The name box was left=386.08, top=349.55, bottom=370.55. The info text box was left=402.08, top=374.55: below the name (374.55 > 370.55) and 16px to the right of the name's left edge. Other rows stayed collapsed. The screenshot shows the text on the line under "Icefish", indented.
- **Outcome:** Pass
