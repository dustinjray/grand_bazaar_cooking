# AC-039: Fish Cake popup row expands to no info

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-007](../user-stories/US-007.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Fish Cake" slot on the Steamed Egg Custard recipe card, clicking the "Fish Cake" row shows exactly "No info available".

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Fish Cake" slot on Steamed Egg Custard, then click the "Fish Cake" row in the popup. Expected: the info text "No info available" is visible for that row.
