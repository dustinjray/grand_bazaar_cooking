# AC-040: Popup fish rows expand independently

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-007](../user-stories/US-007.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Fish A" slot on the Fish & Chips recipe card, after clicking the "Icefish" row and then the "Salmon" row, both rows' info texts are visible ("Seasons: Spring, Winter; Weather: All weather; Locations: Bazaar, Gabriel's House; Rod: Gold" and "Seasons: Autumn, Winter; Weather: All weather; Locations: Mountain Hilltop; Rod: Gold"). Clicking the "Icefish" row again hides the Icefish text while the Salmon text stays visible. Source: https://fogu.com/sos6/activities/fishing.html.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and click the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips. Click the "Icefish" row, then the "Salmon" row, and note the visible info texts. Then click the "Icefish" row again and note them again. Expected: after the first two clicks, both texts are visible. After the third click, only "Seasons: Autumn, Winter; Weather: All weather; Locations: Mountain Hilltop; Rod: Gold" (Salmon) is visible.
