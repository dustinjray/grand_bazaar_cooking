# AC-042: Marbled Eel row expands to its own catch info

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-007](../user-stories/US-007.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Eel" slot on the Unadon recipe card, clicking the "Marbled Eel" row shows exactly "Seasons: Autumn, Winter; Weather: Heavy rain, stormy; Locations: Zephyr Town; Rod: Copper+". It does not show Eel's info text. Source: Marbled Eel row on https://fogu.com/sos6/activities/fishing.html.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Eel" slot on Unadon, then click the "Marbled Eel" row in the popup. Expected: the info text "Seasons: Autumn, Winter; Weather: Heavy rain, stormy; Locations: Zephyr Town; Rod: Copper+" is visible for that row, and Eel's info text is not shown for that row.
