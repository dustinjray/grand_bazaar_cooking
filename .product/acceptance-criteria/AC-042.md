# AC-042: Card fish info hides when the pointer moves away

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-007](../user-stories/US-007.md)

## Criterion

No info text is visible on the Poke card before its "Salmon" slot is hovered. After hovering the slot and then moving the pointer away from it, the info text is no longer visible.

## Verification Method

Start the app and open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked. With the pointer away from all slots, confirm no info text is visible on the Poke card. Hover its "Salmon" slot and confirm the info text appears, then move the pointer to an empty area of the page. Expected: no info text before hovering, the text while hovering, and no info text after the pointer moves away.
