# AC-044: Fish popup rows start collapsed

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-008](../user-stories/US-008.md)

## Criterion

When the popup for the "Fish A" slot on the Fish & Chips recipe card opens, no info text is visible for any row in the popup list.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, and click the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips. Without clicking any row, look at the popup list. Expected: fish rows are listed and no info text is visible in the popup.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us007_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome 154.0.8037.57 via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks; this criterion used its own fresh page load). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips. Without clicking any row, it listed every rendered-visible info text in the popup. Screenshot: `tmp/us007_ac035.png`, checked by eye.
- **Result:** The Fish A popup listed Char, Cherry Salmon, Icefish, Masu Trout, Salmon, Snakehead, and Yellow Perch. Every row was `aria-expanded=false` with its info node hidden. No info text was visible in the popup. The dialog `innerText` contained neither "Season" nor "No info". The screenshot shows names only.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** subagent (US-008)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

### Entry 2

- **Checked:** Orchestrator's own script `tmp/orch_verify_us007.py`. Opened `/?owned=false`, clicked Fish A on Fish & Chips, and listed visible popup info texts without clicking a row.
- **Result:** Rows were Char, Cherry Salmon, Icefish, Masu Trout, Salmon, Snakehead, Yellow Perch. No info text was visible.
