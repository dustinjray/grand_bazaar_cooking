# AC-047: Clicking an expanded popup fish row collapses it

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-008](../user-stories/US-008.md)

## Criterion

In the popup for the "Fish A" slot on the Fish & Chips recipe card, after the "Icefish" row is expanded, clicking that row again hides its info text.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, click the "Icefish" row so its info is visible, then click the "Icefish" row again. Expected: no info text is visible for that row.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us007_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome 154.0.8037.57 via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks; this criterion used its own fresh page load). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, clicked the "Icefish" row so its info was visible, then clicked the "Icefish" row again. Screenshot: `tmp/us007_ac038.png`, checked by eye.
- **Result:** After the first click, Icefish showed "Seasons: Spring, Winter; Weather: All weather; Locations: Bazaar, Gabriel's House; Rod: Gold". After the second click, Icefish was `aria-expanded=false` and no info text was visible for that row. The screenshot shows names only.
- **Outcome:** Pass

### Entry 2
