# AC-049: Crop info still works when stored fish info is removed

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-009](../user-stories/US-009.md)

## Criterion

With the stored fish info removed, hovering the "Turnip" slot on the Turnip Salad recipe card still shows exactly "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe".

## Verification Method

Stop the app, move the stored fish info out of the project, and start the app again. Open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked and hover the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad. Restore the stored fish info afterward. Expected: the info text "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" is visible.

## Evidence

- **Checked:** Stopped the app, moved `data/fish.json` out of the project, and started a new `uvicorn` process serving `app.main:app` on a free loopback port. Ran `tmp/us009_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome over DevTools, real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouse moves). Opened `/?owned=false` and hovered the "Turnip" slot on Turnip Salad. Screenshot: `tmp/us009_ac049_turnip_hover.png`, checked by eye. Restored `data/fish.json` afterward and compared SHA-256 with the backup (`78de8dcf…9865a1`).
- **Result:** Owned only was unchecked. While hovering Turnip, the only visible info text was exactly "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe". No JavaScript exceptions. After restore, `data/fish.json` had the same SHA-256 as the backup (45 species).
- **Outcome:** Pass
