# AC-050: Popup fish rows are collapsed again after reopening

- **Status:** Verified
- **User Story:** [US-008](../user-stories/US-008.md)

## Criterion

After the "Icefish" row is expanded in the "Fish A" popup on Fish & Chips, closing the popup and opening that same popup again shows no info text for any row.

## Verification Method

Start the app, open the recipes page with "Owned only" unchecked, click the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, click the "Icefish" row so its info is visible, close the popup, then click the "Fish A" slot again. Without clicking any row, look at the popup list. Expected: no info text is visible in the popup.

## Evidence

### Entry 1

- **Checked:** Started the app with `python -m app` and ran `tmp/us007_verify.py` (headless Google Chrome 154.0.8037.57 via the DevTools protocol, with real `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` clicks; this criterion used its own fresh page load). On a fresh load of http://127.0.0.1:8000/?owned=false (Owned only unchecked), it clicked the "Fish A" slot on Fish & Chips, clicked the "Icefish" row so its info was visible, closed the popup with Close, then clicked the "Fish A" slot again. Without clicking any row, it listed every rendered-visible info text in the popup. Screenshot: `tmp/us007_ac041.png`, checked by eye.
- **Result:** Icefish was expanded before close. After reopen, every row was `aria-expanded=false` and no info text was visible in the popup. The screenshot shows names only.
- **Outcome:** Pass
- **Recorded by:** subagent (US-008)
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

### Entry 2

- **Checked:** Orchestrator's own script `tmp/orch_verify_us007.py`. Expanded Icefish, closed the popup, opened Fish A again, and listed visible info texts without clicking a row.
- **Result:** No info text was visible in the reopened popup.
