# REQ-001: Show crop info for ingredients on the recipes page

- **Status:** Active

## Description

The recipes page shows a short crop info text for ingredients in two places.

- **Recipe cards:** hovering an ingredient slot button (for example "1× Turnip") shows its info text. The text is hidden when the pointer is not over the slot.
- **Slot popup list:** every item row in the list opened by clicking a slot is collapsible.
  - Clicking a row expands it, showing that item's info text beneath its name, slightly indented. Clicking the row again collapses it.
  - Rows open and close independently.
  - All rows start collapsed every time the popup opens.
  - Hovering a row in the popup shows no info.

The info text follows these rules. The four season pages are fogu's spring, summer, autumn, and winter crop pages (see [REQ-002](REQ-002.md)).

- A crop listed on one or more of the four season pages shows "Season: <seasons>; Purchase at <stores>".
  - Seasons are every season whose page lists the crop, in calendar order (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter), comma separated.
  - Stores are comma separated, and each store appears once, even when several pages list it. If the pages list different stores for the same crop, all of them are combined into the one list.
  - Only store names are shown: no town or bazaar location, prices, or rank.
  - Misspellings from the fan site are corrected: "Happe Shoppe" becomes "Happy Shoppe", and "Miguel's Mercantitle" and "Miguel's Mercantie" become "Miguel's Mercantile".
  - Examples: "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe" (Turnip), "Season: Spring, Summer, Winter; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile" (Potato).
- A rare variety of a crop shows its base crop's seasons and its base crop, with no stores. Example: "Season: Summer, Autumn; Rare variety of Tomato".
- Everything else shows "No info available". This includes group slots such as "Vegetable A" and every ingredient not listed on any of the four season pages.
