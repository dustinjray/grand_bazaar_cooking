# REQ-001: Show crop info for ingredients on the recipes page

- **Status:** Active

## Description

The recipes page shows a short crop info text for ingredients in two places.

- **Recipe cards:** hovering an ingredient slot button (for example "1× Turnip") shows its info text. The text is hidden when the pointer is not over the slot.
- **Slot popup list:** every item row in the list opened by clicking a slot is collapsible.
  - Clicking a row expands it, showing that item's info text beneath its name, slightly indented. Clicking the row again collapses it.
  - Rows open and close independently.
  - All rows start collapsed every time the popup opens.
  - Hovering a row in the popup shows no info.

The info text follows these rules:

- A spring crop shows "Season: Spring; Purchase at <store names, comma separated>". Only store names are shown: no town or bazaar location, prices, or rank. Misspellings from the fan site are corrected (for example "Happe Shoppe" becomes "Happy Shoppe"). Example: "Season: Spring; Purchase at Miguel's Mercantile, Happy Shoppe".
- A rare variety of a spring crop shows its season and its base crop, with no stores. Example: "Season: Spring; Rare variety of Turnip".
- Everything that is not a spring crop and not a listed fish shows "No info available". This includes group slots such as "Vegetable A". Listed fish show catch info as described in [REQ-004](REQ-004.md).
