# REQ-005: Fish info comes from locally stored data

- **Status:** Active

## Description

The fish catch info (seasons, weather, locations, rod level, and time of day when the page gives one) is collected once from fogu's fishing page (https://fogu.com/sos6/activities/fishing.html) and kept in the project. The running app never contacts fogu to show fish info. All 45 species on that page are stored, including fish that are not cooking ingredients today.
