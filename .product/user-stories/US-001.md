# US-001: Hover an ingredient to see its crop info

- **Status:** Deprecated
- **Requirement:** [REQ-001](../requirements/REQ-001.md)

## Story

As the player browsing recipes, I want to hover an ingredient on the recipes page and see its season and which stores sell its seeds (or, for a rare variety, its season and base crop, or "No info available" otherwise) so that I can plan what to plant or buy for the dishes I want to cook without leaving the page.
