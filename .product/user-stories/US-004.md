# US-004: Hover a recipe card ingredient to see its crop info

- **Status:** Complete
- **Requirement:** [REQ-001](../requirements/REQ-001.md)

## Story

As the player browsing recipes, I want to hover an ingredient slot on a recipe card and see its season and seed stores (or its rare-variety base crop, or "No info available") so that I can plan what to plant or buy without leaving the page.
