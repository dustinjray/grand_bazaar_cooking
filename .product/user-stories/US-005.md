# US-005: Click a popup item to expand its crop info

- **Status:** Complete
- **Requirement:** [REQ-001](../requirements/REQ-001.md)

## Story

As the player looking at the items that can fill a slot, I want to click an item in the slot popup to expand or collapse its season and seed-store info (or rare-variety base crop, or "No info available") so that I can read the info reliably inside the popup and compare several items at once.
