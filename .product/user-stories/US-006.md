# US-006: Hover a named fish to see its catch info

- **Status:** Complete
- **Requirement:** [REQ-004](../requirements/REQ-004.md)

## Story

As the player browsing recipes, I want to hover a named-fish ingredient slot on a recipe card and see its seasons, weather, locations, rod level, and time of day when the fishing page gives one (or "No info available" for group slots and non-fish) so that I can plan where and when to catch it without leaving the page.
