# US-006: All four seasons of crop info are stored locally

- **Status:** Complete
- **Requirement:** [REQ-002](../requirements/REQ-002.md)

## Story

As the player running the app on my own machine, I want the spring, summer, autumn, and winter crop info collected once from fogu's four season pages and kept in the project so that the recipes page shows info for every season's crops, including every season a crop grows in, without the app contacting fogu while it runs.
