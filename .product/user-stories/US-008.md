# US-008: Click a popup fish to expand its catch info

- **Status:** Complete
- **Requirement:** [REQ-004](../requirements/REQ-004.md)

## Story

As the player looking at the items that can fill a slot, I want to click a fish in the slot popup to expand or collapse its catch info so that I can read it reliably inside the popup and compare several fish at once.
