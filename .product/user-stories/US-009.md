# US-009: Recipes page keeps working without fish info

- **Status:** Complete
- **Requirement:** [REQ-006](../requirements/REQ-006.md)

## Story

As the player browsing recipes, I want the recipes page to keep working when an ingredient's fish info is missing or the stored fish info is missing or unreadable so that a data gap never stops me from browsing and cooking.
